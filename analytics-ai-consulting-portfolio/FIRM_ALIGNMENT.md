## Why these two cases?
1. Workforce analytics demonstrates descriptive, diagnostic, predictive and prescriptive analysis while making the ethical limits around employee predictions explicit.
2. AI initiative prioritisation demonstrates value hypotheses, readiness, risk, cost, portfolio analysis and transparent budget scenarios.
3. Ollama is local; n8n orchestrates reporting; the language model receives aggregate metrics only. Human review is retained.

All portfolio datasets are synthetic. They do not represent Accenture, Deloitte, EY, their clients or any actual employer.
